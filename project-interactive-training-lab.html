Example project

Unlimited practice that checks every step against the rules.

A student was struggling with step-by-step geometry proofs, where every line has to cite one reason from the teacher’s approved list. DataSail built a training lab that creates new practice problems on demand, checks each step as it is written, and turns progress into a game.

Problem statement

Worksheets run out, and mistakes surface too late.

There were only a few homework problems, the answers were only checked days later, and the hardest part, planning a proof from the end, was never practiced on its own. Help also had to use the teacher’s exact wording, nothing else.

Solution summary

Turn the rulebook into a generator and a checker.

The approved reasons became machine-checkable rules. The tool draws a fresh figure, keeps a problem only if it can be solved with those rules in 3 to 5 steps, and accepts any valid route. A hint ladder and four levels take a learner from guided steps to building the whole proof.

Value realized

Practice on demand, with instant feedback.

The student can practice as much as they want and see right away which line is wrong and why. Ranks and points made practicing something they chose to do, and progress follows them from device to device.

How it generalizes

The same engine fits procedures, compliance and onboarding.

Any skill with a fixed rulebook, such as a safety procedure, a compliance checklist or an approval process, can be trained this way: realistic scenarios generated on demand, every step checked against your exact rules, and progress you can see.

Fidelity Your rules

Only the approved rules are used, word for word, so practice never teaches something the organization wouldn’t sign off on.

Quality Self-tested

Every generated problem is verified as solvable within its limits before a learner ever sees it.

Engagement Ranks

Points, levels and a high-stakes mode keep people coming back, with progress saved per person.
